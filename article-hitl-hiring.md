# Human-in-the-Loop for Hiring

Use AI to organize hiring evidence. Keep the hiring decision with reviewers who can inspect job-related evidence, disagree with a ranking and explain their reasoning.

## Grade the actions

Use these grades as starting points. Sensitive data or combined effects may raise the risk. [Check the grading rule](framework.html#2-grade-the-action).

| Action | Starting grade | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Format a résumé | G1 | Preserve the original. |
| Schedule an interview | G1–G2 | Confirm availability and accommodations. |
| Rank or recommend candidates | G2 or higher | Audit criteria and consequential exclusions. |
| Make a consequential hire or rejection decision | G3 when irreversible and severe | Assign an accountable decision owner. |

## Set the boundaries

Use explicit job-related criteria and evaluate error rates across relevant groups. Remove unsupported proxies for suitability. Provide accommodations and a route to correct inaccurate data. Keeping a human involved does not itself eliminate discriminatory effects.

## Make review useful

Show evidence against each criterion, missing information and model uncertainty. Avoid presenting one opaque score as an objective verdict. Check whether reviewers meaningfully reconsider recommendations.

## Test the workflow

Test incomplete résumés, nonstandard career paths, disability-related accommodations and appeals. Audit outcomes as well as reviewer agreement.

The [EEOC AI and ADA resource](https://www.eeoc.gov/eeoc-disability-related-resources/artificial-intelligence-and-ada) identifies disability-related risks in automated employment tools. Requirements vary by jurisdiction; this action-grading method is not a compliance determination.

Use the [Loop Card and guardrails checklist](kit.html) to record the owner, limits and recovery path.
