G0: Trivial Actions

All three dimensions are 0: no lasting change or easy tested undo, contained reach and trivial stakes. These are LoopRails consequence grades, not universal risk classifications.

Examples

Reading non-sensitive local documentation, drafting text locally or inspecting a disposable fixture. Grades can change with data sensitivity, target environment, authority and cumulative effects.

Choose the control

Allow bounded execution without routine approval. Keep only the records useful for troubleshooting and policy. Reserve interruptions for changes to scope or consequences.

Watch the boundary

Reading secrets or private records is not automatically G0. A read-only production query can expose data, consume resources or invoke effects. Tool names do not settle the grade.

Test it

Restrict inputs and destinations. Confirm that “read-only” really describes the effect. Test that sensitive files and unrelated accounts remain inaccessible.

Check the four RAIL questions: what can be reversed or contained, who is authorized, how it stops and what evidence is recorded. For irreversible effects, document the limit rather than promising undo.

Current computer-use oversight evidence highlights timing and attention problems in human review. It does not determine the correct grade or approval policy for every action.

Use the playbook and Loop Card to capture the design. The research codex preserves the broader evidence and its caveats.
