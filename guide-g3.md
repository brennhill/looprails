# G3: Critical Actions

Reversibility is 2 and either reach or stakes is 2. The action cannot be reliably undone and has external effects or severe consequences. These are [LoopRails consequence grades](framework.html#2-grade-the-action), not universal risk classifications.

## Examples

A destructive production operation without recovery, a consequential irreversible transfer or disclosure of sensitive information. Grades can change with data sensitivity, target environment, authority and cumulative effects.

## Choose the control

Use prevention, explicit authority and independent authorization where required. Stage the action and enforce scope, destination and aggregate limits outside the model. Transfer or prohibit execution when the proposed workflow cannot govern it adequately.

## Watch the boundary

Not every deployment or payment has the same recovery limits. Assess the actual commitment. A confirmation click, model prediction or second-agent agreement does not by itself establish safety.

## Test it

Rehearse failure and incident response. Test unauthorized targets, exceeded limits, reviewer unavailability and cancellation after submission. Record effects that cannot be reversed.

Check the four RAIL questions: what can be reversed or contained, who is authorized, how it stops and what evidence is recorded. For irreversible effects, document the limit rather than promising undo.

Current [computer-use oversight evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04918v2) highlights timing and attention problems in human review. It does not determine the correct grade or approval policy for every action.
