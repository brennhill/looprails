The LoopRails Cookbook

Choose a pattern by the failure you need to fix. Start with a simple baseline, then compare task outcomes, cost and recovery behavior.

Loops

  • Build your first loop: contract, controller and operational limits.
  • Practical loop patterns: coding, data, research and experiment recipes.
  • Verification functions: check coverage and resistance to gaming.
  • Failure recovery: checkpointing, retries and compensation.

Agents

  • Workflow patterns: chaining, routing and parallel stages.
  • Autonomous-agent patterns: planning, tools, reflection and memory.
  • Multi-agent loops: decomposition, handoffs and team-level controls.
  • Context and tools: selective loading and selection accuracy.

Retrieval

  • RAG retrieval patterns: chunking, hybrid search, reranking and access filtering.
  • Advanced and agentic RAG: iterative retrieval and its added failure paths.

Each guide explains the useful role, main limitation and checks to run. The research codex preserves the underlying sources; the evals guide helps compare a change.
