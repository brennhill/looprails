# Human-in-the-Loop for Legal and Contract Work

AI can extract clauses and draft changes. Have qualified reviewers check legal conclusions and client advice. Set clear limits on who can make binding commitments.

## Grade the actions

Use these grades as starting points. Sensitive data or combined effects may raise the risk. [Check the grading rule](framework.html#2-grade-the-action).

| Action | Starting grade | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Summarize authorized documents | Depends on sensitivity | Preserve citations to source text. |
| Draft a clause or redline | G1 | Keep it separate from the executed version. |
| Recommend a consequential legal position | G2–G3 | Require qualified review and current primary authority. |
| Execute or accept a binding agreement | G3 | Use a separately authorized signing process. |

## Set the boundaries

Protect confidential material and restrict signing credentials. Verify quoted clauses against the actual contract and legal citations against current primary sources. Retrieval can return superseded law or a document from the wrong jurisdiction.

## Make review useful

Show the redline, affected obligations, governing jurisdiction and unresolved assumptions. A fluent summary is not evidence that a clause is valid or commercially acceptable. Make the final signer and scope of authority explicit.

## Test the workflow

Seed fabricated cases, omitted exceptions, conflicting versions and unauthorized signature requests. Evaluate the resulting obligations, not just how persuasive the draft sounds.

The [governance references](codex.html#ref-D-1) distinguish oversight from accountability. This page gives workflow design guidance; it does not determine legal duties or replace advice from a qualified professional.
